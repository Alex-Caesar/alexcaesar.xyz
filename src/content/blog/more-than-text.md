_"A picture is worth a thousand words"_ - Fred R. Barnard who was an advertising executive. Are advertisements a piece of art? Art has always been something incredibly interesting to me whether it is in a museum or found in the kitchen or seen in the composition of a mathematical proof. The media medium, style of expression, signature of the creator, and the attempt to capture and communicate meaning is simply fascinating.

[Pre-Historic Cave drawings](https://www.bradshawfoundation.com/chauvet/chauvet_cave_art/index.php) are some of the earliest forms of communication which came before the advancement of written language, and even then there are so many beautiful levels of expression and culture. I would encourage you to take a moment to check out [this crazy discovery about the Cosquer cave](http://archeologie.culture.gouv.fr/archeo-sous-marine/en/cosquer-cave) and imagine what a distinctly different yet strangely familiar world our ancestors lived in. I wonder what our future successors will relate to and be confused about from the artifacts which survive the weathering of time. Most likely, they will find scattered heaps of plastic tupperware and wonder who thought it was a good idea to mass-produce a chemical that we have no known means of properly disposing of.

All of this is to say that I added an image and an icon to the website.
